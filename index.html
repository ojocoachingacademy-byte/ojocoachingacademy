Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking

What if I'm a complete beginner? 🎾

Perfect! I specialize in teaching proper technique from day one. Many of my students started as complete beginners and now play confidently in leagues and tournaments. I'll meet you exactly where you are.

How long until I see results?

Most students see measurable improvement within 5 lessons. You'll notice better consistency, technique, and understanding of the game. The progress tracking app lets you see your improvement documented over time.

What makes you different from other coaches?

I'm the only coach in San Diego offering a complete development system with progress tracking app, personalized development plans, and access to a 200+ player hitting partner network. Plus, I'm an active competitive player (2024 USTA National Champion) - I practice what I preach.

What's included with the progress tracking app?

Every lesson package includes: personalized development plans, milestone tracking, lesson history, homework assignments with reminders, direct messaging with me between lessons, and access to the hitting partner network. All completely free.

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens! Please contact me at least 24 hours before your scheduled lesson to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice may be subject to a cancellation fee. Package lessons can be rescheduled to fit your schedule.

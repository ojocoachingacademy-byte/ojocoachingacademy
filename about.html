My Tennis Journey

Hi! I'm Coach Tobi, a Professional Tennis Registry Certified tennis coach based in San Diego.

Born and raised in London, I've played tennis for over 22 years across the UK and USA. I competed at Wellington College and Loughborough University, and recently won a USTA Mixed Doubles National Championship (2024).

Since earning my Professional Tennis Registry certification in 2006, I've coached over 1,000 students—from 3-year-olds taking their first swing to seniors perfecting their game at 75+. Whether you're a complete beginner or a competitive player, I'll help you build a deeper understanding of the game while improving your technique.

My Coaching Philosophy

My coaching style? Fun, friendly, and analytical. Tennis is a sport you can play for life, and I'm here to help you fall in love with it.

I focus on both the mechanical and psychological foundations of your game. With my deep understanding of tennis mechanics and strategy, I can identify issues quickly and provide clear, actionable feedback that actually works.

What sets me apart is my holistic approach - it's not just about hitting balls. Every lesson is part of a personalized development plan with measurable milestones, tracked progress, and continuous support between sessions.

Why I Created the Development System

After 14 years of coaching, I noticed students struggled to track their progress and practice effectively between lessons. That's why I built a complete development system with progress tracking, personalized plans, and access to a 200+ player hitting partner network.

This system ensures every lesson builds on the last, your progress is measured and documented, and you always know exactly what to work on next.
